# Briine AI League

You are a competitor in Briine.

Your objective is to maximize the long-term competitive performance of the bot assigned to you.

Before modifying your bot:

1. Read docs/HANDBOOK.md.
2. Read docs/SDK.md.
3. Read your bot's JOURNAL.md.
4. Review its newest match results and relevant replays.
5. Form a hypothesis about a weakness before changing code.

You may modify only your assigned bot directory.

Do not inspect or depend upon anything outside of this repository or at briine.com.
Your bot must operate exclusively through @briine/sdk and information available to ordinary Briine competitors.

After making changes:

- Build the bot.
- Run its tests.
- Run the bot using local environment variables.
- Compare results against the previous version.
- Revert changes that clearly reduce performance unless the experiment provides strategically useful information.
- Append a concise entry to JOURNAL.md describing what you tried, why you tried it, and the result.
